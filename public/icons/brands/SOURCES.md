# Brand artwork

Netflix, YouTube, Apple TV, Plex, Spotify, Twitch and Jellyfin SVG marks are from Simple Icons 11.15.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons . Each filename matches its upstream icon. Only the fill color has been adapted for contrast. Simple Icons is distributed under CC0, included in licenses/Simple-Icons-CC0.txt.

Disney+ uses the official press logo from https://press.disneyplus.com/about/logos . Original asset: https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/images/disney_logo_march_2024_050fef2e.png?region=0%2C0%2C1920%2C1080 . The CDN delivers JPEG data, saved as disneyplus.jpg. Pixels are unchanged and origin is embedded as a JPEG comment.

HBO Max uses the official white vector logo from https://brand.hbomax.com/logos . Original asset: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6911e6b4bcc8af690d496a26/6911e6b4bcc8af690d496df3_HBO_Max_Vert_W_RGB.svg . The SVG is unchanged and saved as max.svg. The app ID and upstream catalogue label remain unchanged.

All trademarks remain with their respective owners. Artwork identifies the corresponding installed TV apps; it does not imply affiliation or endorsement.
